# Delete Your Data — Clavex

> Last updated: July 2026

#Overview

> Clavex stores all data locally on your device.
> There is no server-side account to delete.

---

## How to delete all your data

### Option 1 — Uninstall the app (recommended)

Uninstalling Clavex permanently and irreversibly deletes:

| Data | Result |
|------|--------|
| All saved passwords | ✅ Permanently deleted |
| Your PIN | ✅ Permanently deleted |
| App settings | ✅ Permanently deleted |
| Local database | ✅ Permanently deleted |

**Steps:**
1. Long press the Clavex icon on your home screen
2. Select **Uninstall**
3. Confirm — all data is immediately and permanently deleted

### Option 2 — Reset from within the app

If you want to keep the app but start fresh:
1. Open Clavex
2. Tap the menu in the top right corner
3. Select **Sign out**
4. On next launch, create a new PIN

> ⚠️ This does not delete your existing encrypted database file.
> For complete data removal, uninstall the app.

### Option 3 — Contact us

If you have any concerns about your data or need assistance:

📧 **Email:** [clavex.help@gmail.com]  
**Subject:** Data deletion request

We will respond within 30 days.

## What we cannot delete

Since Clavex collects no personal data, there is nothing 
stored on our servers to delete. The only external data associated 
with your use of the app is:

- **Anonymous crash reports** (Firebase Crashlytics) — 
  automatically deleted after 90 days. No personal identifier 
  is attached to these reports.
- **Advertising interactions** (Google AdMob) — 
  managed by Google's own privacy policy. 
  You can opt out via your device's advertising settings.

---

## Before you delete

We strongly recommend **exporting your passwords** before 
uninstalling the app, as there is no way to recover them afterward.

**How to export:**
1. Open Clavex
2. Tap the menu
3. Select **Export passwords**
4. Choose a secure export password
5. Save the `.pwdvault` file in a safe location
